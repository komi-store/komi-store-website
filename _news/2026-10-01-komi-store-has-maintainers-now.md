# Komi Store has maintainers now

In July I wrote that [Komi Store needs maintainers](https://komistore.app/blog/komi-store-volunteer-hire/). The project had grown too big for one person.

Thank you to everyone who applied. Three people are now joining the team.

## illumiat

[illumiat](https://github.com/illumiat) works on updates, making sure new releases show up in the app. illumiat also fixes bugs across the whole app.

## YumeYuka

[YumeYuka](https://github.com/YumeYuka) works on Android, mostly on how apps get installed.

## KelvinCrag

[KelvinCrag](https://github.com/KelvinCrag) takes care of the community: answering questions, sorting bug reports, and keeping things friendly.

More people on the team means faster replies on issues and pull requests.

Say hi to them on GitHub or [Discord](https://discord.komistore.app).
